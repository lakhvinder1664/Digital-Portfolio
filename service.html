[ COMMON QUESTIONS ]

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical website project take?

+
A focused landing page or one-page website usually takes between 3 to 5 business days. Full multi-page business websites or custom WordPress builds typically take 1 to 2 weeks, depending on content readiness and custom feature complexity.

Can you convert my existing Figma or Adobe XD design into code?

+
Yes, absolutely! Figma to code is one of my primary specialties. I ensure exact 1:1 pixel accuracy, identical typography scaling, fluid responsive breakpoints, and smooth micro-animations.

Will I be able to update content, images, and text myself?

+
Yes! For WordPress websites, you will have a user-friendly admin dashboard with Elementor where you can update text, images, blog posts, and products without writing any code. For custom HTML sites, I structure the content cleanly and can provide a short walkthrough.

What do you need from me to get started on a project?

+
Just a brief overview of your business goals, any reference websites whose design or vibe you like, your branding assets (logo, preferred color scheme), and the text/media you want included. If you don't have all the content yet, I can assist with wireframing the structure first.

Do you offer post-launch support and maintenance?

+
Yes. Every project includes complimentary post-launch support to resolve any minor tweaks or questions. I also offer monthly website maintenance for continuous updates, speed monitoring, and new section additions.
